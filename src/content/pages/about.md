Red Clay Sangha's mission is to bring the dharma to all beings, individually and collectively.  Our practice is to live the Bodhisattva ideal of returning again and again - to this space, to our community, to this life - until the suffering and dis-ease that is so pervasive in the world comes to an end. We welcome you, just you, individually you, not so much regardless of your faith, tradition, culture, but precisely because of what makes you uniquely "you". We invite you to explore how the teachings and practices of Buddhism, as we bring them forth at Red Clay Sangha, can help you.

![A dharma talk at Red Clay Sangha](/images/pages/rcs-pres-young.jpg)

Our offerings are led primarily by our Guiding Teacher and other experienced Red Clay Sangha dharma practitioners, with occasional talks and retreats from visiting teachers (see "[Teachers](/about/people/)" page for more). We value the breadth and diversity of our teaching, but above all we value the safety of our members and visitors, and we hold ourselves and our teachers to the highest [ethical conduct and precepts](/documents/standards-of-ethical-conduct.docx).

We believe strongly in the core value of generosity and abundance and to the extent possible, we offer our practice programs without fees. It is not always possible to offer programs for free (for example residential retreats are expensive to run), but where we must charge, we keep fees to the minimum possible.

See our weekly programs [here](/programs/).
