### I. Dharma Talk Recordings
Most dharma talks are recorded and made available on our YouTube channel here link [here](https://www.youtube.com/@redclaysangha). Playlists of talks for each of the Thursday evening topics are available on this channel and also listed on the [Thursday event page, here](/events/6246874/). Recent Sunday talks are generally linked to from our home page list, and others available on the YouTube channel. Retreat and other special event talks are often not recorded, and recording errors result in gaps in the sequence, so if you're looking for a specific talk and can't find it, that might be why. Below is a list of select talks and some highlighted playlists.
