### Why Become a Member?
Joining Red Clay Sangha is a way to make a commitment to the mission and people of Red Clay Sangha and the Atlanta community, and to formalize a sense of belonging for oneself and for others.

Members of Red Clay Sangha are entitled to elect and be elected to the board of directors at the annual membership meeting, approve the annual budget (if this function is delegated by the board), and participate in annual planning sessions to ensure that the organization remains responsive to members’ needs and desires.

Individuals become members of Red Clay Sangha on the basis of having a sense of belonging, which will generally express itself any of the following three ways: (1) attending programs and activities; (2) volunteering time or skills to support the operation of the sangha, or (3) making a financial donation. If you are interested in becoming a member, please follow the process below.

### How Do I Become a Member?
To become a member, click the button below to go to the [New Member Profile](https://redclaysangha.org/New-Member-Profile), and complete the form. (Note: There is no required donation to become a member, as described above, but if you do wish to make a donation, please indicate the amount on the form and go to the [Donate](/donate/) page on the website to make your donation (as a lump sum or in installments).)
