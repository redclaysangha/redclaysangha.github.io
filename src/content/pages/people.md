## Guiding Teacher

![Gareth Young](/images/pages/2020-gareth-1.jpg)

Gareth Young, Red Clay Sangha's Guiding Teacher, holds teaching authority in the Thai Forest lineage of Ajahn Chah, Jack Kornfield and Lisa Ernst and has trained extensively in Chan and Zen; his teaching integrates the three, with the Pali suttas and Chan sources as its primary ground. He lives in the Atlanta area with his wife and their dogs, cat, chickens and bees. He has two adult children. 

Gareth holds regular and ad hoc private practice discussions with individuals. Please [contact Gareth by email](mailto:gareth@redclaysangha.org) if you would like to schedule a conversation.

## Red Clay Sangha Teachers

![Anand Garlapati](/images/pages/anand.jpg)

**Anand Garlapati**

**Anand** has been practicing at Red Clay Sangha over 5 years. Anand works in information technology. He is born as a Hindu and familiar with Sanathana Dharma, Karma yoga and Bhagavadgita since childhood, and influenced by the doctrine of non-violence laid out by Buddha, Jesus Christ, Mahatma Gandhi, Martin Luther King, and others. Anand received the precepts from Red Clay Sangha’s guiding teacher Gareth Young in 2023. Anand has been experimenting with dharma and Karma yoga.

Katie Rhodes is a member and teacher with Red Clay Sangha. She has been a Buddhist practitioner for over 30 years, primarily in the Insight/Vipassana tradition. She has been practicing Insight Dialogue meditation since 2023 and is a member of the Northeast Insight Dialogue Teachers Cohort. She facilitates an Insight Dialogue practice group for Friends of Red Clay. Katie has a doctorate in clinical social work. She uses mindfulness-based cognitive approaches in her work as a psychotherapist. Her meditation and practice with dharma supports her commitment to build strong spiritual friendships in compassionate community.

## Visiting Teachers

![Dhammadipa](/images/pages/ayya-dhammadipa-2024.jpg)

**Dhammadipa** is the founder of Dassanaya Buddhist Community. She has been practicing Buddhism since 1987. She is a Dharma Transmitted Teacher in the Suzuki Roshi lineage of Soto Zen and was previously ordained in the Theravada tradition for 5 years. In addition to English, Dhammadīpā teaches in Spanish, an expression of her Latin heritage. She is an author whose work has appeared in the Buddhist publications “Lion’s Roar” and the “Buddhadharma” magazine. Dhammadīpā is the mother of a lovely adult daughter, and enjoys sewing and watercolor painting.

Learn more about her and her teachings at: [Dassanaya.org](http://Dassanaya.org)

![Ben Connelly](/images/pages/authorphotobenconnelly2018edit.jpg)

**Ben Connelly** is a Soto Zen teacher and Dharma heir in the Katagiri lineage. He also teaches mindfulness in a wide variety of secular contexts including police training and addiction recovery groups. He works with multifaith groups focused on intersectional liberation, racial justice, and climate justice. Ben is based at Minnesota Zen Meditation Center, travels to teach across the United States, has written for Tricycle and Lion’s Roar magazines, and is author of *Inside the Grass Hut, Inside Vasubandhu's Yogacara, Vasubandhu's "Three Natures",* and *Mindfulness and Intimacy.*

## Governance of Red Clay Sangha

Red Clay Sangha has three layers of governance: a board of directors responsible primarily for administrative matters; a guiding teacher responsible for dhamma and programming matters; and an Elder's Council, which serves primarily as a consultative body for the guiding teacher, but its members are also available on an as needed basis for members and sangha participants who wish to consult on a sensitive or personal matter.
