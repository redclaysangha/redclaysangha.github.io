Donations to Red Clay Sangha cover the cost of our practice spaces and programs to bring the dharma to the greater Atlanta community. Red Clay Sangha is a volunteer-supported organization, and no one receives any compensation, including our Guiding Teacher. We are currently in the market for a larger physical space, so anticipate a higher overhead to support that, and are also accumulating a building fund. You can make a donation by PayPal, by check or by credit card. If you need help, please send an email to [info@redclaysangha.org](mailto:info@redclaysangha.org).

We deeply appreciate your gifts.
