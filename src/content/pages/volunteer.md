title: "Volunteering at Sangha"
---

Red Clay Sangha is your sangha. By volunteering you play an important role in building the sangha you want and a supporting network of relationships for yourself. You are also fulfilling Buddhist teachings by putting self-interest aside and placing yourself in service to others, an act of generosity and kindness. And engaging in the work itself, bringing mindfulness and intention to bear, focused on the activity and not the outcome, you are engaging in practice.

If you're in a place where this feels like something that might make sense, please consider completing the Volunteer Interest Form (link below). To help inform you, take a look at the list of volunteer opportunities below. And remember: it's important not to overwhelm ourselves or to turn work into a distraction

[Sangha Volunteer Interest Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScvGmlySBRvNzHtRoB9xm6bfHTA-vLgtEg1jtMuBf--9lu5dA/viewform)

## Volunteer Opportunities

Opportunities for volunteering include the following. 

- **Upkeep of the facility and meditation space**: Including cleaning, maintenance and supplies
- **Sunday service support**: Altar flowers, altar supplies, tidying
- **Newsletter:** Supporting and expanding sangha communications
- **Social Events and Activities:** As the Buddha told Ananda, "The Sangha is the whole practice". 
- **Calendar management:** Coordinating space use and users, prioritizing, etc.
- **Website:** Helping to keep the website current, accurate, and informative 
- **Social Media:** Managing and supporting our social media accounts
- **Fundraising:** If you have expertise in fundraising, we would love your help!
